## Intended use (from the public 510(k) summary)

Somryst is a prescription-only digital therapeutic intended to provide a neurobehavioral intervention (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia - CBT-I) in patients 22 years of age and older with chronic insomnia. It is a 9-week prescription digital therapeutic delivered in six cores (sleep restriction, stimulus control, cognitive restructuring, sleep hygiene, relapse prevention), with a clinician dashboard.
