# K233577 — Sleepio®

- Applicant: Big Health, Inc.
- Product code: QVO
- Decision: 2024-08-05 (Substantially Equivalent)
- Regulation: 882.5801 (Class 2)

## Intended use (from the public 510(k) summary)

Sleepio is a digital therapeutic intended for the treatment of chronic insomnia / insomnia disorder as an adjunct to usual care in patients aged 18 and older. It is a prescription device delivering CBT-I and can be made available on the order of a licensed healthcare provider. Predicate: Somryst (K191716).
