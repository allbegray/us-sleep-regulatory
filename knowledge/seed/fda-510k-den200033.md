# DEN200033 — NightWare Kit, Apple iPhone, Apple Watch, Apple iPhone Charging Cable, Apple Watch Charging Cable

- Applicant: Nightware, Inc.
- Product code: QMZ

## Intended use (from the public 510(k) summary)

NightWare is a prescription device that detects and interrupts nightmares in patients with nightmare disorder or PTSD-related sleep disturbance. It is the De Novo authorization behind product code QMZ (21 CFR 882.5705). It is not a general insomnia CBT-I device.
